1. **BLOCKER: The closed Garage overlay steals touches from the Water button, completely breaking the "splash" step of the core loop.**
   * **Why it matters:** A 3-year-old tapping the giant glowing Water button gets zero response because `.garage-side-col` and `.garage-bottom-bar` explicitly set `pointer-events: auto` even when `#garageOverlay` is closed (`opacity: 0`).
   * **Exact change:** In the `<style>` block for `#garageOverlay`, add `visibility: hidden;` and remove `pointer-events: auto;` from `.garage-side-col` and `.garage-bottom-bar`. Instead, scope child interactivity to the open state only: `#garageOverlay.open { visibility: visible; pointer-events: auto; opacity: 1; transform: scale(1); }` and `#garageOverlay.open .garage-side-col, #garageOverlay.open .garage-bottom-bar { pointer-events: auto; }`.

2. **The truck gets trapped at the world perimeter and behind hills because perimeter hills have no collision and boundary bounces only nudge the angle slightly.**
   * **Why it matters:** A 3-year-old holding a finger in one spot will drive straight into the fence or inside a perimeter hill mesh, losing sight of their truck and getting stuck outside the core loop.
   * **Exact change:** In Section 4 (`hillPositions.forEach`), register every hill as a physical obstacle: `obstacles.push({ x: hp.x, z: hp.z, r: hp.r * 0.85, mesh: hm });`. In Section 13 (`animate`, under `if (distFromCenter > WORLD_RADIUS)`), replace `fireTruck.angle += 0.6;` with a soft steer-back toward the town center and inward velocity push: `const toCenterAngle = Math.atan2(fireTruck.x, fireTruck.z); fireTruck.angle = toCenterAngle; fireTruck.x = nx * (WORLD_RADIUS - 1.5); fireTruck.z = nz * (WORLD_RADIUS - 1.5); fireTruck.hopVel = 6.5;`. Apply the exact same `WORLD_RADIUS` clamp and `obstacles` collision loop to `tankerTruck` in Section 13 so Parent 1 cannot drive off the island either.

3. **Trees, houses, and flames positioned south of the truck (`z > fireTruck.z`) block the camera's view of the truck during spraying.**
   * **Why it matters:** When the child stops on the north side of a burning tree or house, the prop and flame sit directly between the fixed-angle camera and the truck, hiding the truck, its water stream, and its celebratory hop.
   * **Exact change:** In Section 13 (`animate`, under `SMOOTH CAMERA FRAMING`), raise the camera angle and pull back slightly when close to a target flame (`const flameCamBoost = (targetFlame && targetDist < 16) ? 4.5 : 0;` added to `desiredCamPos.y`), and add an occlusion check in `animate` that pushes any tree/house/flame between the camera and `fireTruck` (`ob.z > fireTruck.z && Math.abs(ob.x - fireTruck.x) < 4.5 && (ob.z - fireTruck.z) < 14`) to scale down its `y` or fade material opacity to `0.35` until the truck drives clear.

4. **The child's Fire Truck only sprays in a forward cone unless within `9.5` units, while the Water button starts pulsing at `16.5` units.**
   * **Why it matters:** Between `9.5` and `16.5` units, the UI tells the 3-year-old "TAP WATER NOW!" (`pulse-glow`), but if the truck is facing away or sideways (`dot <= -0.15`), the water shoots into empty grass instead of hitting the flame, breaking cause-and-effect.
   * **Exact change:** In `handleWaterSpray()` (Section 12), remove the directional cone restriction (`if (dot > -0.15 || dist < 9.5)`) so that whenever `dist < 17.5`, the water stream always auto-aims at `flame` and gently rotates `fireTruck.angle` toward `Math.atan2(-(flame.anchor.x - fireTruck.x), -(flame.anchor.z - fireTruck.z))` (`fireTruck.angle += diff * dt * 5.0`).

5. **Kitten rescue stops forever after 3 rescues, removing the best emotional reward in the game after ~2 minutes of play.**
   * **Why it matters:** Saving the kitten and hearing it meow is the peak reward of the core loop for a 3-year-old; once `rescuedKittens.length === 3`, `spawnFlame()` never spawns a kitten again for the rest of the session.
   * **Exact change:** In `spawnFlame()` (Section 7) and `extinguishFlame()` (Section 12), remove the `rescuedKittens.length < 3` cap on spawning `activeTreeKitten`. In `extinguishFlame()`, when `rescuedKittens.length >= 3`, still allow rescuing the tree kitten: have the newly rescued kitten hop onto the truck, do a big celebratory meow + 3-star drop, and cycle/replace the oldest ladder kitten mesh (or have all 3 ladder kittens jump and meow in unison).

6. **Kitten-in-a-tree is visually tiny and silent until rescued, so the child doesn't notice a kitten needs help.**
   * **Why it matters:** A 0.42-radius sphere tucked beside a 2.6-unit flame on top of a tree reads as a small blob on an iPad; without an audio-visual callout, the child treats it like a normal flame.
   * **Exact change:** In `spawnFlame()` (Section 7, under `if (isKittenFlame)`), scale the tree kitten up (`kMesh.scale.setScalar(1.45)`), play `playMeowSound()` when the truck gets within `20` units of `activeTreeKitten` (in `animate`), and make `activeTreeKitten.group` hop up and down (`activeTreeKitten.group.position.y = activeTreeKitten.anchor.y - 0.2 + Math.abs(Math.sin(now * 6)) * 0.45`) while facing the camera.

7. **Cooperative "Team Splash" is too easy to miss because normal flames die in `0.74s` and family flames aren't prioritized when parents join.**
   * **Why it matters:** At `hp: 1.0` and `1.35/s` damage per hose, two players spraying together extinguish a flame in ~0.35 seconds—before the child even registers that they are spraying together—and `isFamily` flames only spawn 28% of the time.
   * **Exact change:** In `spawnFlame()` (Section 7), increase `isFamily` probability to `0.65` whenever `tankerTruck.active || heli.active` is true, and set `isFamily` HP to `3.0`. In `applyWaterToFlame()` (Section 12), when 2+ players have sprayed the same flame within the last `1.0s`, emit continuous rainbow/gold sparkles (`0xfff200`) on the flame *while* spraying so the child feels the cooperative boost before the final pop.

8. **Washed-out pastel lighting and low-contrast toon shading dull the visual reward of flames, water, and rainbows.**
   * **Why it matters:** Toddlers respond to high-contrast, candy-bright primary colors; the current `HemisphereLight` (`0.78`), bright toon shadow band (`145`), and warm fog (`0xffd8b8`) flatten the 3D toy look and make celebratory rainbows blend into the sky.
   * **Exact change:** In Section 3, change `toonGradData` shadow/mid values from `145, 210, 255` to `85, 185, 255` for punchy cel-shading; lower `hemiLight.intensity` to `0.5` and raise `dirLight.intensity` to `0.95`; reduce `scene.fog` density from `0.0042` to `0.0018` with color `0x68d8ff`; and thicken `addOutline` default scale from `1.07` to `1.09` with pure black (`0x11151c`).
